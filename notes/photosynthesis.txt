PHOTOSYNTHESIS - Biology Notes

What is photosynthesis?
Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants, algae and some bacteria make their own food
using light energy. It mainly happens in the leaves, inside tiny structures called chloroplasts.
Chloroplasts contain a green pigment called chlorophyll, which absorbs light (mostly red and blue light)
and reflects green light. That is why plants look green.

The word equation:
carbon dioxide + water --(light energy, chlorophyll)--> glucose + oxygen

The balanced chemical equation:
6CO2 + 6H2O --> C6H12O6 + 6O2

Raw materials (inputs):
- Carbon dioxide enters the leaf from the air through small pores called stomata.
- Water is absorbed by the roots and carried up to the leaves through the xylem.
- Light energy comes from the Sun.

Products (outputs):
- Glucose is the food made by the plant. It is used for energy (in respiration), stored as starch,
  or turned into cellulose to build cell walls.
- Oxygen is released into the air through the stomata as a by-product.

The two stages:
1. Light-dependent reactions happen in the thylakoid membranes of the chloroplast. Light energy splits
   water molecules (photolysis), releasing oxygen and producing the energy carriers ATP and NADPH.
2. The light-independent reactions (the Calvin cycle) happen in the stroma. They use ATP and NADPH to
   turn carbon dioxide into glucose. These reactions do not need light directly.

Limiting factors (things that can slow down photosynthesis):
- Light intensity
- Carbon dioxide concentration
- Temperature (enzymes work best at an optimum temperature, about 25-35 degrees C for many plants)

Why is it important?
Photosynthesis produces the oxygen that most living things need to breathe, and it is the starting
point of almost every food chain on Earth. It also removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

Photosynthesis vs respiration:
Respiration is roughly the reverse of photosynthesis: glucose + oxygen --> carbon dioxide + water + energy.
Plants carry out respiration all the time, day and night, but photosynthesis only happens when there is light.
